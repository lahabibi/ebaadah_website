# Ebaadah SEO Website Architecture

## Goal

Build the website as the acquisition layer for Ebaadah: rank for useful Islamic app and worship-support searches, earn trust before app-store launch, and guide visitors toward app downloads, email notification, or support.

## Phase 1: Launch Foundation

- `/` - Home: brand promise, app preview, core features, store CTAs, trust signals.
- `/features` - Features overview: prayer times, Quran, Qibla, duas, habits, reminders.
- `/support` - Support and contact.
- `/privacy` - Privacy policy for app-store review and user trust.
- `/terms` - Terms of service.

## Phase 2: Feature SEO Hubs

- `/prayer-times` - Accurate prayer times app, calculation settings, reminders.
- `/quran` - Quran reading, listening, bookmarks, notes, study, progress.
- `/qibla` - Qibla direction compass and location-based guidance.
- `/duas` - Daily duas, morning/evening adhkar, travel, sleep, hardship, gratitude.
- `/daily-habits` - Worship habit tracking, consistency, prayer completion, Quran goals.

## Phase 3: Search Content Library

- `/blog` - Guides and articles.
- `/blog/best-prayer-times-app` - Commercial-intent app comparison page.
- `/blog/how-to-pray-on-time-consistently` - Habit-building guide.
- `/blog/how-to-find-qibla-direction` - Qibla evergreen guide.
- `/blog/morning-evening-adhkar-guide` - Duas/adhkar evergreen guide.
- `/blog/quran-reading-habit` - Quran consistency guide.
- `/blog/ramadan-preparation-app` - Seasonal Ramadan acquisition page.

## SEO Rules

- One primary keyword per page.
- Each page needs a clear H1, meta title, meta description, canonical URL, and internal links.
- Use `https://www.ebaadah.com/` as the canonical host unless the domain redirect strategy changes.
- Sitemap should include only canonical URLs that are ready for indexing.
- App-store CTAs stay disabled or "Coming soon" until the App Store and Google Play URLs exist.

## Conversion Goals

- Pre-launch: email notification signups.
- Launch: App Store and Google Play clicks.
- Growth: blog-to-feature-page journeys, feature-page-to-store clicks, support/privacy trust checks.
